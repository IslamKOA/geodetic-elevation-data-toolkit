# Authors and contributions

**Geodetic & Elevation Data Toolkit (v5.0.0)**

The software authors, in citation order, are:

1. **Islam Koa** — First author, lead developer; software design, implementation, coding, testing, and maintenance. Institut des Géosciences de l’Environnement (IGE), INRAE / Université Grenoble Alpes; An-Najah National University.
2. **Alain Recking** — Co-author; scientific supervision and project management (not software coding). Institut des Géosciences de l’Environnement (IGE).
3. **Laurent Borgniet** — Co-author; scientific supervision and project management (not software coding). Laboratoire des EcoSystèmes et des Sociétés en Montagne (LESSEM).

**Recommended citation:** Koa, I., Recking, A., & Borgniet, L. (2026). *Geodetic & Elevation Data Toolkit* (Version 5.0.0) [Computer software]. https://github.com/IslamKOA/geodetic-elevation-data-toolkit

For shortened in-text citations: Koa et al. (2026).

The attribution above distinguishes software development from scientific supervision; it does not imply that the supervisors wrote the source code.
